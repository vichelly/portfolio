## Why

The world has three problems that compound into "it feels buggy."

**The parkour detour is the source of the camera and control bugs.** It is the only reason the world carries a two-curve containment system (`containmentFor`, `clampFor`, `projectToDetour`, `Containment`, `avatarState.containment`), and that system is what freezes `avatarState.t` on entry, forces `CameraRig` to branch its forward vector, and lets the arena's 26-unit containment radius reach into neighbouring plazas. The previous change patched the camera symptom; the branch itself remains, costing a second 481-sample per-frame curve projection, a second clamp path, a dynamic import, and a whole capability's worth of surface area — for an optional side activity that carries no career content. Removing it deletes the bug class rather than treating it.

**Things visibly stop appearing on screen.** `World.tsx` wraps `DetourSign`, `SkillMotes`, every mounted `Station`, and `ParkourCourse` in **one** `<Suspense fallback={null}>`. Any single child that suspends blanks the entire boundary: every plaza, every signpost, and every skill mote vanish at once. This fires routinely, because walking forward mounts a new `Station` whose `PlazaBadge` calls `useTexture` on a logo that has never been fetched (`/logos/itau.jpg`, `fei.jpg`, `fiap.jpg`, …), and because toggling the language re-creates all ~100 `Text` instances in `SkillMotes`. That is the reported "buga e para de aparecer as coisas na tela."

**Half the trail has no shadows and the avatar has no contact shadow.** The `directionalLight`'s shadow camera is a fixed ±70 box around the world origin with the default target at `(0,0,0)`, while the trail runs from z ≈ +16 to z ≈ −140. Everything past roughly z ≈ −80 — the `fiap`, `certifications`, and `contact` plazas — casts and receives nothing. The `ContactShadows` plane is likewise pinned at `[0, 0.02, 0]` with `scale={9}`, so the avatar's grounding shadow only exists near the intro plaza and disappears for the rest of the walk. Combined with a `far: 260` camera plane that clips geometry the `fogExp2` density of `0.0068` has not yet faded out, the world visibly degrades the further the visitor walks — exactly when it should be at its best.

**It is slow on other machines, and the scene is built in a way that guarantees it.** `Decor` renders roughly 250 scattered props as individual React elements — and a `Tree` is three separate meshes — so on the order of 350–400 draw calls of scenery exist, all `castShadow`, all mounted for the entire length of the trail with no distance windowing, and therefore all submitted twice per frame (shadow pass and colour pass). `SkillMotes` adds 66 individually-materialled `Text` meshes on top, also mounted for the whole trail. `ContactShadows` runs with `frames={Infinity}`, so it re-renders a depth pass of the scene and blurs it every single frame — to draw a shadow that, as noted above, is pinned at the origin and invisible for most of the walk. On top of that, `detectTier()` awards the `high` tier to any viewport ≥ 1200px wide regardless of the actual GPU, so an integrated-graphics laptop gets a 2048 shadow map, a `dpr` cap of 2, 300 dust motes, full decor density, and the full post-processing stack with 4× multisampling; `PerformanceMonitor` can only walk that back one tier at a time, after the visitor has already had a bad first impression.

Meanwhile the main trail is a flat corridor with nothing to do but hold a key, so the jump the avatar already has is never used outside the detour that is being removed.

## What Changes

- **Remove the parkour capability entirely.** Delete `components/world/parkour/`, the detour curve and every detour-only export in `lib/world/trail.ts`, `avatarState.containment`, the world store's `containment` slice, the detour signpost, the detour accent, and the detour strings. `CameraRig` loses its branch and always derives forward from the trail tangent; the character controller always clamps to the trail and always advances `avatarState.t`. **BREAKING** (internal only): the `Containment` type, `clampFor`, `containmentFor`, `projectToDetour`, `clampToDetour`, `detourHalfWidth`, `DETOUR_*`, and `DETOUR_ACCENT` are removed.
- **Add simple, optional obstacles to the main trail.** A small data-driven set of low hop-over blocks, stepping stones, and a low beam, placed between plazas (never inside a plaza, never in a panel or signpost footprint), registered through the existing `solids` registry the same way the parkour platforms were. Every obstacle is sized against the avatar's real jump arc (apex ≈ 1.74 units, comfortable gap ≈ 2.5 units) **and leaves a clear walkable lane beside it**, so a visitor who cannot or will not jump can always walk around and still reach every plaza. Obstacles are scenery with collision, not a gate.
- **Fix the disappearing content.** Give each `Station` its own `Suspense` boundary instead of one shared boundary, preload the plaza logo textures and the two fonts before the world mounts, and window `SkillMotes` the way stations are windowed so the mote set is not rebuilt wholesale on a language switch.
- **Fix the lighting that stops working.** Make the sun's shadow camera follow the avatar along the trail instead of sitting fixed at the origin, and replace the origin-pinned `ContactShadows` with a grounding shadow that travels with the avatar. Bring the camera's `far` plane and the fog density into agreement so nothing is clipped before it has faded.
- **Make it run on ordinary hardware.** Draw the scattered decor as a handful of `InstancedMesh` batches (one per prop primitive) instead of hundreds of individual meshes, window decor and skill motes by distance along the trail the way stations already are, stop `ContactShadows` from re-rendering every frame, shrink the sun's now-following shadow camera so it buys more resolution for less cost, and make the quality tiers honest — `high` must require real evidence of a capable GPU, not merely a wide window, and the step-down must react before the experience has already stuttered.
- **Smooth the motion.** Soften the camera's position and look-target damping (currently `1 - pow(0.001, δ)`, effectively a snap), cross-fade stations in and out over their presence value instead of popping them at the mount-window edge, and add hysteresis to the integer-percent mount window so a station cannot flicker at a boundary.
- **Directed visual polish.** Tighten the existing dawn-in-a-valley palette and materials in place — plaza and trail surface contrast, accent emissive levels, signpost and pylon readability against the warm ground — without changing the art direction.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
(none)

### Modified Capabilities
- `portfolio-world`: the narrative-order requirement drops its parkour-detour clauses; the adaptive-quality requirement gains a floor — the world must hold an interactive frame rate on integrated graphics, and the tier chosen must reflect measured capability rather than viewport width alone; a new requirement makes the world's visual system hold along the **whole** trail (shadows, grounding, and distance handling must not degrade with distance from the origin); and a new requirement forbids a single loading asset from removing unrelated already-placed content from the scene.
- `avatar-control`: the "Jump is available everywhere" requirement drops its reference to the parkour area; a new requirement covers the trail obstacles — they are jumpable, and every one of them is bypassable on foot.
- `parkour-challenge`: **removed in full.** The capability and all six of its requirements cease to exist.
