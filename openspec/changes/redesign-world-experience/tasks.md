### Notes from the desktop walkthrough

Two real bugs were found and fixed beyond what the tasks anticipated:
1. Skill motes rendered as flat, camera-fixed text, so from most angles they read mirrored (a self-rotating "Java" mote came out as "svaL"-shaped). Fixed by billboarding each mote to the camera every frame instead of self-rotating on Y.
2. The repositioned `certifications` plaza (`[7, -106]`) landed inside the parkour arena's `DETOUR_ARENA_RADIUS` (26 units) after the trail was reshaped for the new anchor layout, so standing at that plaza reported "Parkour detour" instead of "Certifications." Fixed by moving the detour arena's end point further from both `fiap` and `certifications` (`lib/world/trail.ts`'s `DETOUR_CURVE`, now ending at `[32, -90]` with margins verified > `DETOUR_ARENA_RADIUS` to every plaza anchor).

One content item needs the user's own confirmation before shipping: the AWS certification entry (`lib/content/experience.ts`, id `aws-certifications`) is titled generically ("AWS Certifications (Practitioner, Associate, Professional)") because the exact exam names/levels could not be confirmed from the filenames on the LinkedIn profile alone.
